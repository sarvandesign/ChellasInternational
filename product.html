Coco Peat Blocks

Coco peat is a natural, eco-friendly growing medium made from coconut husk fiber. It enhances water retention, aeration, and root development—ideal for horticulture and hydroponics. Widely used in global agriculture, we supply export-grade coco peat in various forms.

Coir Bale

A coir bale is a compressed bundle of coconut husk fibers used in horticulture, agriculture, and industrial products. It is sustainable, biodegradable, and provides excellent moisture retention and aeration for plants. Coir is ideal for soil conditioning, hydroponics, and erosion control.

Coir Disk

A coir disk is a compact, round disc made from coir fibers, typically used in gardening and horticulture. When soaked in water, it expands to provide a growing medium for plants, offering excellent water retention and aeration. Coir disks are often used for seed starting, hydroponics, or as a soil amendment due to their eco-friendly, biodegradable nature.
